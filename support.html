Linework

Support

Linework helps you get good at drawing by practising the fundamentals every day with Apple Pencil: confident lines, ellipses, perspective, boxes and cylinders, in that order.

Getting started

  • Open the app and tap Start today's session. Each day you get a warm-up, the next step on your roadmap, a review and a fun drawing.
  • Draw with Apple Pencil. Fingers never leave ink: pinch to zoom, twist to rotate the page, two-finger tap to undo, three-finger tap to redo.
  • Tap Check to get a score and one line on what to improve. Marks shows the ideal lines over your drawing.
  • Double-tap (Apple Pencil 2) or squeeze (Apple Pencil Pro) to switch between pen and eraser.

Common questions

My finger doesn't draw. That's on purpose: Linework is Pencil-only so your palm and fingers can rest on the screen.

Can I skip ahead? Yes. Settings → Open all units.

Where are my drawings? Each exercise's History shows every attempt, and Play drawings are in the Sketchbook on the home screen. Everything stays on your iPad.
