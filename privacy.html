Linework

Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

Linework is an iPad app for practising drawing fundamentals, made by Devvik Agarwal. The short version: everything you draw stays on your iPad, and the developer never receives any of your data.

What Linework collects

Nothing. Linework has no account, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code, and it never connects to the internet.

What Linework stores on your iPad

Your drawings, the scores and feedback the app works out on the device, your daily practice history and your settings. They are stored only on your iPad and included in your own device backups (iCloud or computer), which Apple handles under its own privacy policy. The developer can't see them.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, Linework schedules it on your iPad. It's created and delivered by iPadOS on the device; no information is sent anywhere.

Deleting your data

Settings → Reset all progress deletes every drawing and score. Deleting the app removes everything Linework stored.

Children

Linework collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
