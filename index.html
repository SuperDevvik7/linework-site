Linework

Drawing fundamentals, a little every day

Linework is an iPad app for practising the fundamentals of drawing with Apple Pencil: confident lines, ellipses, perspective, boxes and cylinders. Each day gives you a short session, and every exercise is scored on your iPad with one clear note on what to fix.

It's in beta on TestFlight.
